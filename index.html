Welcome to Zakat Calculator

Zakat is an obligatory form of charity and one of the Five Pillars of Islam. It helps purify a Muslim’s wealth and supports people in need. In general, zakat becomes due when your zakatable wealth reaches the nisab (minimum threshold) and you have held that wealth for one lunar year. A common rate is 2.5% of eligible wealth.

When does gold or silver reach nisab?

Nisab is the minimum amount of wealth that makes zakat potentially due.

✦

Gold

87.48 g

Commonly used threshold: 7.5 tola of gold, or its cash value.

◈

Silver

612.36 g

Commonly used threshold: 52.5 tola of silver, or its cash value.

Scholarly methods differ: some use 85 g of gold and 595 g of silver. Jewelry rulings and whether to use the gold or silver value can also vary. These weights are reference points; other zakatable assets such as cash may count toward the nisab.
